What you get

Built for NABH Entry-Level (2nd edition). Preparing for full accreditation (6th edition)? Tell us on the call.

  • Equipment register

    Each machine you list or photograph, department by department, in your own asset codes (or codes we suggest if you have none). Each fact shows where it came from.

  • PM (preventive maintenance) tracker

    Planned and done, month by month, for medical equipment and for utility, facility and furniture items.

  • Calibration register

    Due and done dates for each machine in the register, with the certificates you have.

  • Critical equipment and downtime

    Your critical-equipment list, and a ready-to-use downtime sheet that runs from each complaint until the ward confirms the fix.

  • Complaint log

    A ready-to-use log: when each complaint came in, who took it, what was done and when the ward confirmed it.

  • Training and on-call sheets

    Ready-to-fill sheets: who is trained on which model, and who to call for repairs at night and on weekends.

  • Licence calendar and contracts

    The licences you tell us about, with renew-by dates from your certificates, and your AMC or CMC contracts with what each covers.

  • Equipment SOPs

    Short SOPs in Word that you can edit, each with a header for document number, version and approvals.

  • QR label sheet

    A label sheet you print on A4, one label for each machine in your register.

Delivered as Word and Excel files you can edit, plus a PDF copy that names who prepared and reviewed it, and the date.

How it works

For a hospital with about 250 to 300 machines.

  1. Day 0 A 20-minute video call

    Keep three nameplate photos handy: we read them live and show you how each machine will look in your register. You name one coordinator and pay in advance.

  2. Day 1 First photos, first sample

    Your coordinator sends the first photos and any old Excel files, the way we agreed on the call. We send back a 10-machine sample within 24 hours.

  3. Weeks 1 to 2 Photo walk, ICU and OT first

    One photo of each nameplate and one of the whole machine, department by department. Your staff also find invoices, AMC contracts and certificates, and we read them.

  4. Week 3 Draft and review

    A draft file, a short list of what is still missing, and your label sheet, then a 30-minute review call.

  5. Weeks 3 to 4 Final file

    Your quality coordinator checks 10 machines on the floor and signs, your administrator accepts the register, and your staff stick and scan the labels.

Plan on about 3 to 4 weeks and about 11 to 15 hours of your staff's time, mostly taking photos and finding papers. Expect a second photo pass for hard-to-read nameplates, plus time to stick the labels. Your draft arrives within 48 hours of the last photo.
